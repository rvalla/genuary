I have committed the worst sin
a man can commit. I have not been
happy. May the glaciers of oblivion
sweep me away and lose me, merciless.

My parents engendered me for the game
risky and beautiful game of life,
for the earth, the water, the air, the fire.
I let them down. I was not happy. Fulfilled

was not their young will. My mind
applied itself to the symmetrical porphyries
of art, which interweaves nothings.

Courage was bequeathed to me. I was not brave.
It does not abandon me. It is always by my side
The shadow of having been a wretch.
