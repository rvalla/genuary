No one will have failed to observe that the floor often folds in such a way that one part rises at right angles to the plane of the floor and then the next part is placed parallel to this plane to give way to a new perpendicular conduct which is repeated in a spiral or broken line up to extremely variable heights By bending down and placing the left hand on one of the vertical parts and the right hand on the corresponding horizontal part, one is in momentary possession of a step or rung. Each of these steps formed by two elements is placed somewhat higher and forward than the previous principle that gives meaning to the staircase, since any other combination will produce forms perhaps more beautiful or picturesque but incapable of moving from a first floor to a second floor.
The stairs are climbed from the front because backwards or from the side they are particularly uncomfortable The natural attitude consists of remaining standing the arms hanging without effort the head upright although not so much that the eyes stop seeing the steps immediately superior to the one that is stepped on and breathing slowly and regularly To climb a stair one begins by raising that part of the body located to the right below almost always wrapped in leather or chamois and that except for exceptions fits exactly in the step Placed in the first step this part which we will call foot for short, the equivalent part on the left is picked up, also called foot, but which should not be confused with the foot mentioned above, and taking it to the height of the foot, it is followed until it is placed on the second step, so that the foot will rest on the second step and the foot will rest on the first step The first steps are always the most difficult until the necessary coordination is acquired The coincidence of name between the foot and the foot makes the explanation difficult Take special care not to lift both the foot and the foot at the same time.
Arrived in this way to the second step it is enough to repeat the movements alternately until you reach the end of the staircase It is easy to get out of it with a slight heel strike that fixes it in its place from which it will not move until the moment of descent
